RZRFM: Football Management Sim

Take charge of a club in a living football world. Build the squad, set the tactics, run the finances and stadium, and write your own career story, season after season.

Join the Discord Support

What you do

  • Pick a club in one of several original leagues and work toward the board's expectations.
  • Scout, negotiate and sign players. Shape your staff, training and youth teams.
  • Choose formations and tactics, then follow every match day.
  • Grow the stadium and facilities and balance wages against Gate Income.
  • Chase league titles, cups and continental glory.

Free to play, with optional purchases. Every club, player and competition is fictional.
