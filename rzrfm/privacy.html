Privacy Policy

RZRFM: Football Management Sim · Effective 15 November 2026

RZRFM is made by rezazr, an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the game stores, why, where, and the choices you have. Questions: rezazramadan1+games@gmail.com.

Short version

  • No sign-up. We never ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or location.
  • Your careers and settings stay on your device.
  • To keep Glory, the premium currency, safe and fair, the game uses a random account on our server. That account is not linked to your name.
  • Google Play or the App Store handles payments. We never see your card or payment details.
  • We do not sell your data, and the game has no analytics or tracking tools of its own.

1. Data stored on your device

Career saves, autosaves, settings (language, text size, sound) and a signed copy of your Glory wallet are stored in the game's private storage on your device. We cannot read them. Your phone's own backup, Android Backup or iCloud, may include them under your Google or Apple account. Uninstalling the game deletes them from the device.

2. Your Glory account on our server

The first time the game connects to the Glory service, it creates an account made of a random ID and a random secret. The ID and secret are stored on your device, so your Glory follows you after a device restore. The server keeps:

3. Device integrity checks

To stop cheating and fraud, the game asks Google Play Integrity on Android or Apple App Attest on iPhone to confirm that the app is genuine and runs on a real device. Google or Apple processes device information to answer. We receive only the result and use it to accept or refuse the connection.

4. Purchases

Glory is bought through Google Play Billing or Apple In-App Purchase. They collect your payment details under their own privacy policies. We receive the purchase receipt, verify it with Google or Apple, grant the Glory, and receive refund notices so refunded Glory can be removed. Each order carries a code derived from your random account ID so that a purchase reaches the right account.

5. Optional rewarded videos

Some versions of the game offer optional videos that earn a reward. You choose whether to watch them. When they are available they are served by Unity LevelPlay and its advertising partners. To show, limit and measure ads, these may collect device identifiers such as your advertising ID, your IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads, under their own policies (see the Unity game player privacy policy). You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. On iPhone, the game asks your permission before any tracking across other companies' apps and websites. If your version shows no videos, no advertising code collects anything.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent personal data.
  • We collect no name, email, phone number, contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera data.
  • The game has no analytics or crash-reporting tools of its own. Google Play and Apple may give us anonymous, aggregated crash and performance reports if you allowed them in your device settings.

7. Children

RZRFM is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has used a purchase or the Glory service, contact us and we will delete the account.

8. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Device data: delete saves in the game, or uninstall the game.
  • Server account: email rezazramadan1+games@gmail.com with the subject "RZRFM data deletion". If you bought Glory, include the Google Play order number (it starts with GPA.) or your App Store order ID, so we can find the account. We delete the account and its Glory within 30 days. Purchase records that the law requires us to keep are kept only for that purpose. Deleted Glory cannot be restored.
  • If you never bought Glory, the server holds only a random ID and game balances that cannot be linked to you.

Depending on where you live, for example under Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law, the EU or UK GDPR, or US state laws, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your data, and to complain to a data protection authority. Email us to use them.

9. Security and transfers

All connections use HTTPS. Account secrets are stored only as one-way hashes, and server access is limited to the developer. Data is stored in Indonesia. Google, Apple and advertising partners may process it in other countries under their own safeguards.

10. Changes

We will update this page and its effective date when the game's data use changes, and give notice in the game or on this page for important changes.
