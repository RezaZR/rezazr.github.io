Common questions

I bought Glory but it did not arrive

Keep the game open with an internet connection. Purchases that were interrupted are delivered again when the game reconnects to the store, and restarting the game retries them. If Glory is still missing after a day, email us the order number (Google Play orders start with GPA.).

How do I get a refund?

Payments are handled by the store. For Google Play, use your Play order history. For the App Store, use reportaproblem.apple.com. Glory from a refunded purchase is removed from your wallet.

Will my careers and Glory move to a new phone?

Careers are saved on your device. Your Glory account is linked to the game installation, not to a login. Both are restored when you set up the new phone from a backup of the old one (Android Backup with the same Google account, or an iCloud or encrypted computer backup on iPhone). Without a backup, email us with a purchase order number and we will help recover purchased Glory.

Is RZRFM free to play?

Yes. Everything in the game can be played without paying. Glory can also be earned by playing. Paid help has fixed, stated effects and limits, and it never decides a match or a trophy on its own.

Are the clubs and players real?

No. Every club, player, stadium and competition is fictional. Any resemblance to real people or clubs is unintended.
